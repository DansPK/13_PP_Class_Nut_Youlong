Bot: If the printer is jammed, follow these steps:

1. Turn off the printer by pressing the power button located on the top or front of the device.
2. Open the printer’s access panel (usually found on the top, front, or back) by pressing the release latch or sliding it open to gain access to internal mechanisms.
3. Carefully inspect the internal components and identify any jammed paper. Gently pull out visible paper scraps or torn pieces, being cautious not to touch sensitive parts with oily skin.
4. Check for obstructions in the paper path, including the paper tray, rollers, and print head. Remove any dust, dirt, or debris that may be contributing to the jam.
5. If the paper tray was dislodged during the jam, realign it according to the manufacturer’s instructions and ensure it clicks securely into place.
6. Check and clean the print head for blockages or dried ink using a lint-free cloth or cotton swab, following the manufacturer's guidelines.
7. Reassemble the printer in reverse order of disassembly, ensuring all panels and doors are securely closed.
8. Turn the printer back on and test it by printing a test page or document. If issues persist, refer to the manufacturer’s troubleshooting guide or contact the IT helpdesk for further assistance.
